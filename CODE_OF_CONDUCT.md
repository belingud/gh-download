# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

This project expects respectful, direct, and constructive participation.

Examples of welcome behavior:

- Focus on the technical issue and the user impact.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about bugs and risks.
- Provide reproducible details when reporting a problem.
- Respect that maintainers may keep the project small and decline broad changes.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Posting credentials, tokens, or private repository data in public
- Repeatedly pushing unrelated changes after maintainers have declined the scope

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may close issues or pull requests that violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may lead to blocking from the repository.
